Status: proposed

## Problem

need actual public method use and business results beyond successful image creation.

## Decision
Preserve exact source/runtime/provider receipts for every named method and three
fresh documented alternatives, independently check returned data/artifacts, and
confirm exact owned cloud/local resource absence. Retain operator failures separately.

## API Design
No runtime or public interface changes. Enumerate9/8/8/4/6 named methods and existing
health/discovery; scoped business return values remain unchanged. No optional raw TCP
forwarding, model, benchmark leaderboard or unpublished package capability invented.

## Alternatives considered

reward cannot establish each template's requested observable business result.

## Dependencies
Refs https://github.com/Alibaba-Agent-Sandbox/awesome-templates/issues/244 and
https://github.com/Alibaba-Agent-Sandbox/blogs/issues/16. Source evidence precedes

benchmark/SDK work, literal CLI/full unchanged SDK/direct HTTP, independently decoded
PNG or exact downloaded bytes/native trajectory replay, fresh state and owned cleanup.
Local document, record, secret/diff checks and current-head CI remain required.

after their own gates. Overall50 remains open; vendor credentials, browser-model/IDE
work and managed interpreter defect do not inherit these five scoped passes.
